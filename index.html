WHAT TO THINK ABOUT

Outside: cameras should watch approaches before someone reaches a door or window.

Water: protect appliances and plumbing areas. A leak sensor detects the problem; a shutoff can reduce the damage.

Life safety: smoke, CO and fall protection can be just as important as intrusion protection.
