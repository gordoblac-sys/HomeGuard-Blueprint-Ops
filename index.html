DESIGN HINTS

Outside: cameras need a view of approaches, driveways, doors, windows or the backyard.

Water: leak sensors belong low near plumbing and appliances. A shutoff valve belongs near the main water supply.

Life safety: smoke, CO and fall protection should compete for budget with burglary protection.
